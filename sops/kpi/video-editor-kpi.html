🎬 Video Editor KPI

Key Performance Indicators – How We Measure Success

📊 Tracked Daily 🎯 Output + Quality 📈 Weekly Review

📝 KPI Overview

These KPIs measure both output (did the video ship) and quality (was it better than the original, did it hook in 3 seconds). Tracked daily, reviewed weekly with the Delhi Team.

🎯 Key Performance Indicators

📅 Daily Short Output

1 / day
At least one short video shipped per editor, per day
(Shorts Delivered / Working Days) ≥ 1

🎬 Long Video Cadence

1 / 3 days
One long video delivered every 3 days when assigned
(Long Videos / 3-Day Blocks) ≥ 1

🔬 Viral-Source Accuracy

100%
Every video based on a proven viral outlier (not an average video)
(Viral-Sourced / Total Videos) × 100

🥇 Beats-Original Rate

100%
Edit judged better than the original creator's edit
(Beats-Original / Total Videos) × 100

⚡ 3-Second Hook Pass

100%
Hook stops the scroll within the first 3 seconds
(Hook-Pass / Total Videos) × 100

🎞️ Animation Density

High
Heavy, motion-rich editing — no static, animation-light edits
Reviewer rating: animation-rich = pass

📊 Reporting Compliance

100%
Each video logged with its source viral link in the daily report
(Logged / Delivered) × 100

♻️ Revision Rate

< 1 / video
Low rework — quality is right the first time
Avg revisions before approval
