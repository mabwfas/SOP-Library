🛡️ Fiverr Risk Manager KPI

Key Performance Indicators – How We Measure Success

📊 Tracked Daily 🎯 Zero-Tolerance Metrics 📈 Weekly Review

📝 KPI Overview

These KPIs measure the effectiveness of the Fiverr Account Risk Manager across all 4 accounts. Performance is tracked daily and reviewed weekly with Shreyansh and the Delhi Team.

🎯 Key Performance Indicators

💬 Message Response Rate

100%
All messages replied within 24 hours across all accounts
(Replied Messages / Total Received) × 100

📦 On-Time Delivery Rate

100%
Zero late deliveries — all at-risk deliveries flagged within 48 hrs
(On-Time Deliveries / Total Deliveries) × 100

🔔 Notification Clearance

100%
All notifications from past 24 hrs checked and cleared daily
(Cleared Notifications / Total) × 100

📊 Daily Report Submission

100%
Report submitted to Shreyansh & Delhi Team every working day
(Reports Sent / Working Days) × 100

⭐ Review Response Rate

100%
Reviews given back for every buyer who left a review
(Responses Given / Reviews Received) × 100

⚠️ Low Rating Escalation

< 15 min
Time to escalate 4-star or lower reviews to Shreyansh
Avg time from review detection to escalation

🎫 Cancellation Ticket Rate

100%
Support ticket filed for every cancelled order
(Tickets Filed / Cancelled Orders) × 100

🔔 Brief Response Rate

100%
All matching briefs responded to with portfolio pitch
(Briefs Responded / Total Matches) × 100

🚩 Delivery Flag Accuracy

100%
All deliveries due within 48 hrs flagged and manager notified
(Flagged At-Risk / Total At-Risk) × 100

🕒 Clock Clearance (24h)

0 clocks
No clock icons on any unseen message in the past 24 hours, any account
Count of clock icons across all accounts (target = 0)

🎯 Sub-4.7★ Rating Removal

100%
Every rating below 4.7★ resolved with client and removed from account
(Low Ratings Removed / Low Ratings Received) × 100

🤝 Mutual Cancellation Rate

100%
Every cancellation is mutual (or fully refunded) — zero non-mutual
(Mutual Cancellations / Total Cancellations) × 100

📋 Account Care / SOP Compliance

100%
Each account managed per the Risk SOP & KRA — daily care checklist met per account
(Accounts Fully Compliant / Accounts Managed) × 100
