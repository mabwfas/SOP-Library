🎬 Video Editor Daily Task Sheet

Minimum: 1 short video shipped today (long video = 1 every 3 days)

🔬 Research Block — Find a Proven Winner

  • Open the creators' Instagram profiles we follow / take concepts from
  • Find their average views (e.g., baseline ~1,000 views)
  • Find the viral outlier — the video that hit 100k/400k while the rest do ~1k
  • Lock the concept & script — same concept, same script, same everything
  • Pull the script via ChatGPT (script from completed video) or NotebookLM (paste the creator's link → script back)

🎬 Production Block — Our Avatar

  • Generate the avatar video on HeyGen using our avatar + the winning script
  • Download the raw avatar video
  • Plan the edit — hook in first 3 seconds, animation beats, psychology hits
  • Gather assets — B-roll, motion graphics, sound

✂️ Edit & Ship Block — Better Than the Original

  • Edit top-notch — heavy animations, relatable, nothing left lacking
  • 3-second hook check — does it stop the scroll? (the main game)
  • Beats-original check — is our edit better than theirs?
  • Deliver the finished short
  • Log it — video + the source viral link in your daily report

💡 Pro Tip

The hook is the whole game. If the first 3 seconds don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters. Recreate proven winners, never gamble on unproven ideas.
