🎯 Role Overview & The Goal

🎬 What a Digital Heroes Video Editor Does

You take AI avatar videos generated from HeyGen (or our Avatar Video tool) and turn them into scroll-stopping, viral-grade short videos using your editing skills. We do not invent from zero — we recreate videos that have already proven they go viral, using our own avatar, and we edit them better than the original creator did.

The Goal (Read This Twice)

  • If a video has gone viral, we make our own version of it with our avatar — same concept, same script, same everything.
  • Our edit must be better than the original editor's. Always. That is the standard.
  • Top-notch, relatable, animation-rich, psychology-driven, and a hook that stops the scroll in 3 seconds.

🚨 The Bar Is Non-Negotiable

Editing must be top-notch and cannot be left lacking anywhere. If a video's editing is not better than the original and does not meet this bar, that is a direct failure of the role. Not meeting this goal can lead to termination — we will not continue working with editors who do not hit this standard. This is stated plainly so there is no confusion.

🎬 The Core Job — Step by Step

# Step What To Do
1 🔬 Pick a proven viral video Use the Research Method (Section 4) to find a video that has clearly gone viral on a creator's profile we follow.
2 📝 Extract the script Pull the exact script of that viral video using ChatGPT or NotebookLM (see Section 5). Same concept, same script, same everything.
3 🤖 Generate our avatar video Generate the video on HeyGen / our Avatar Video tool using our avatar and that script. Download the raw avatar video.
4 ✂️ Edit it better than the original Edit with top-notch skills: strong 3-second hook, heavy animations, relatable pacing, human-psychology-driven structure. The final must beat the original edit.
5 ✅ Deliver & report Deliver the finished short and log it in your Daily Task Sheet / daily report (which viral video it was based on + the link).

📅 Daily Commitment (Mandatory)

🚨 Minimum Output — Not Optional

Every person on the video editing team has a daily commitment. This is the floor, not the target. "Just one" is the minimum — more is always welcome.

Video Type Commitment Rule
🎞️ Short video / reel 1 per day One short video (or one reel) per person, per day. A short video = 1 day of work.
🎬 Long video 1 every 3 days A long video gets 3 days for one video. Still a continuous commitment — one long video must land every 3 days.

⚠️ Report Every Day

Even on a day inside a 3-day long-video build, you still report progress. Zero-output days without a reported reason are a violation.

🔬 The Research Method (Be Exact — No Guessing)

🎯 Exactly What We Are Looking For

This is specific on purpose. Do not "find something good." Follow this exactly so there is zero confusion about what counts.

  1. Go to the Instagram profile of the creators we follow / take concept ideas from.
  2. Find their average views. Scan their grid — what does a normal video do? Example: if almost every video sits around 1,000 views, that is their baseline / average.
  3. Find the outlier that went viral. On that same account, look for the video that massively beat the average — e.g., baseline is ~1,000 views but one video hit 100k or 400k views. That spike = proven viral concept.
  4. Take THAT video. The one that went to 100k/400k while the rest do ~1k. Use its same concept, same script, same everything for our own avatar.
  5. Edit it better than them. Our version must out-edit the original creator's version — every single time.

💡 Why This Works

A video that beat its own account's average by 100x has already proven the concept and script work. We are not gambling on ideas — we are recreating pre-validated winners with our avatar and superior editing.

🏆 The Quality Bar — Top-Notch or Out

Standard What It Means
⚡ 3-Second Hook THE MAIN GAME The video must hook and stop people from scrolling within the first 3 seconds. This is the single most important thing.
🥇 Better Than Original Our edit must be better than the original creator's edit — always. If it is not, the job is not done.
🎞️ Heavy Animations A lot of animation is required — motion, text, transitions. Static cuts are not enough.
🧠 Human Psychology Editing decisions must be driven by human psychology — retention, curiosity loops, pattern interrupts, payoff.
❤️ Relatable The final video must feel highly relatable to the viewer — not corporate, not stiff.
✨ Nothing Left Lacking Top-notch everywhere — audio, captions, timing, color, pacing. No weak spots.

🚨 Consequence

If this bar is not achieved, expect termination — we will not continue with editors who do not deliver top-notch work. This is the goal we want from you, stated clearly.

7

📜 Do's & Don'ts

✅ DO's

  • Recreate proven viral videos (100k+ outliers) with our avatar
  • Keep the same concept & script that already went viral
  • Out-edit the original creator — every time
  • Hook in the first 3 seconds
  • Use heavy animations + psychology-driven pacing
  • Hit the daily commitment (1 short/day, long = 3 days)
  • Use ChatGPT / NotebookLM to pull the exact script
  • Log every video + source link in the daily report

❌ DON'Ts

  • Don't copy average videos — only proven viral outliers
  • Don't change the winning concept/script
  • Don't ship an edit weaker than the original
  • Don't bury the hook — never a slow start
  • Don't deliver static, animation-light edits
  • Don't miss the daily commitment without a reported reason
  • Don't leave any part of the edit "good enough" — top-notch only
