🎬 Video Editor KRA

Key Result Areas – What You're Responsible For

🎯 Viral Recreation ⚡ 3-Sec Hook 📅 Daily Output

📝 Role Overview

The Video Editor recreates proven viral videos using our own avatar (HeyGen / Avatar Video) and edits them better than the original creator. You own the hook, the animation, the psychology, and the daily output.

🚀 Primary Mission: Turn pre-validated viral concepts into top-notch avatar shorts that stop the scroll in 3 seconds — every single day.

🎯 Key Result Areas

🔬 1. Viral Research

  • Track creators' Instagram profiles
  • Identify average vs. viral-outlier views
  • Pick only proven 100k+ outliers
  • Same concept, same script

🎬 2. Avatar Recreation

  • Generate our avatar on HeyGen
  • Reuse the exact winning script
  • Pull scripts via ChatGPT / NotebookLM
  • Download & prep for edit

✂️ 3. Editing Excellence

  • Better than the original — always
  • Heavy animation & motion
  • Top-notch audio, captions, color
  • Nothing left lacking

⚡ 4. Hook & Psychology

  • 3-second scroll-stopping hook
  • Curiosity loops & pattern interrupts
  • Highly relatable to the viewer
  • Retention-driven structure

📅 5. Daily Output

  • 1 short video per day (minimum)
  • Long video = 1 every 3 days
  • Report progress daily
  • No silent zero-output days

📊 6. Logging & Reporting

  • Log each video + source viral link
  • Track in the Daily Task Sheet
  • Flag blockers early
  • Maintain a delivery trail
